How SDM Turns PCM into Pure 1-Bit DSD.

Standard DAC chips rely on tiny, power-constrained internal interpolators. AMR moves the math onto Apple Silicon (AMX FP64 & Metal GPU), converting multi-bit PCM into a 2.82–12.29 MHz (DSD64–DSD256) 1-bit pulse-density stream that drives your DAC's direct DSD analog path.

01 • Oversampling

64×–256× FP64 Filter

Raises PCM into MHz rates via 65,536-tap AMX or 262,144-tap Metal GPU FIR filters with -3.5 dB headroom and True-Peak ISP guard.

02 • Noise Shaping

7th-Order 7EC Loop

Each sample's 1-bit error is fed back (z⁻¹) through 7 integrators with Chebyshev zeros, sweeping noise out of 20 Hz – 20 kHz above 100 kHz.

03 • Look-Ahead

Viterbi Trellis (M=16)

Evaluates 16 parallel bit paths ahead with an ISI transition penalty, eliminating limit-cycle idle tones and midrange glare.

04 • Direct DAC Path

Lossless DoP v1.1

Packs raw DSD64–DSD256 bits into 0x05/0xFA integer frames (705.6/768 kHz Hog Mode) so your DAC's analog filter reconstructs music directly.

Technical FAQ.

Why use AMR instead of AirPlay 2 or Apple's iTunes Remote app? +
macOS locks CoreAudio to a fixed sample rate in Audio MIDI Setup, while AirPlay 2 often transcodes Apple Music to 256 kbps AAC or caps resolution at 48 kHz. AMR plays Apple Music directly on your Mac wired to your DAC (or streams 24/192 WAV to BluOS/DLNA) while giving every phone, tablet, and TV a sub-15ms OLED controller.
Does AMR install virtual audio drivers like BlackHole? +
No. AMR taps audio via macOS Sonoma/Sequoia's native AudioHardwareCreateProcessTap API with lock-free C11 SPSC ring buffers—zero kernel extensions or virtual audio cables.
When should I choose 7EC DSD DoP vs 768 kHz HQ PCM NOS (LNS15)? +
Choose HQ PCM NOS (LNS15) for R2R ladder DACs (Holo Audio, Denafrips, Musician, Schiit) to eliminate imaging aliases and drop audible noise below -168 dBFS. Choose 7EC DSD64–DSD256 DoP for USB DACs and DDCs with direct 1-bit DSD paths (Topping, SMSL, Matrix, Chord).
How does hardware SoundCheck work without digital volume loss? +
AMR keeps macOS output locked at 0.0 dBFS and adjusts Rotel, Denon/Marantz, Hegel, NAD, or Lyngdorf attenuators over TCP using studio iTunNORM metadata, live BS.1770 LUFS, and psychoacoustic Crest Compensation.
