            Engineering FAQ
          

          
Frequently Asked Technical Questions.

        


        

          

            
              Why do audiophiles use AMR instead of AirPlay 2 or Apple's iOS iTunes Remote app?
              +
            
            

              macOS locks CoreAudio to a single static sample rate in Audio MIDI Setup, resampling your tracks unless switched manually. Meanwhile, AirPlay 2 from iOS/macOS frequently transcodes Apple Music streams to lossy 256 kbps AAC or caps resolution at 44.1 / 48 kHz, and Apple's legacy iTunes Remote app only browses local files—it cannot search the Apple Music streaming catalog, launch personalized stations, inspect the signal path, or switch DACs. AMR runs Apple Music natively on your Mac wired directly to your DAC (or streams uncompressed 24-bit / 192 kHz WAV to BluOS/DLNA) while giving every phone, tablet, and TV a sub-15ms OLED remote.
            

          


          

            
              Does AMR install kernel extensions or virtual audio drivers like BlackHole?
              +
            
            

              No. AMR uses macOS Sonoma/Sequoia's native AudioHardwareCreateProcessTap CoreAudio API paired with custom C11 lock-free atomic SPSC ring buffers (CAtomicPrimitives). Zero virtual audio devices, kexts, or system-wide sound routing hacks are installed, and when Pre-DSP / Hybrid DSP is turned off, 100% pure hardware passthrough is active.
            

          


          

            
              How does AMR's 4096-point FFT Spectral Verifier spot fake "Hi-Res" tracks?
              +
            
            

              SpectralIntegrityAnalyzer performs continuous Hann-windowed vDSP_fft_zrip spectral analysis and bitwise Least Significant Bit (LSB 0..7) scanning directly on the PCM stream. If a track delivered in a 24-bit / 96 kHz or 192 kHz container exhibits a steep brickwall cliff at 22.05 kHz or 24.0 kHz, or has its bottom 8 bits filled with zeros, AMR flags it as FAKE HI-RES • 22K CUT, UPSAMPLED 48K, or 16-BIT PADDED and caches the verdict for instant recall.
            

          


          

            
              What DACs benefit most from HQ PCM NOS (LNS15) vs DSD64–DSD512 DoP?
              +
            
            

              Discrete R2R / Multibit ladder DACs (such as Holo Audio Spring/May, Denafrips Ares/Pontus/Venus/Terminator, Musician Pegasus, and Schiit Multibit) shine with HQ PCM NOS up to 705.6 / 768 kHz paired with the 15th-order Lipshitz LNS15 noise shaper (< -165 dBFS noise floor, 27+ effective bits). USB DACs and DDCs with dedicated direct 1-bit paths (Topping, SMSL PO100 PRO over I2S/S/PDIF, Matrix, Chord) can engage DSD64–DSD512 DoP v1.1 driven by AMR's 7th-order 7EC modulator with Viterbi Trellis M=16 look-ahead and ISI regularization.
            

          


          

            
              How does External Amplifier TCP SoundCheck normalize tracks without breaking Bit-Perfect?
              +
            
            

              Digital volume scaling destroys bit-perfection (and corrupts 0x05/0xFA DoP markers). AMR keeps macOS output pegged at 100% (0.0 dBFS) and connects over TCP to Rotel (:9590), Denon / Marantz (:23), Hegel (:50001), NAD (:30000), or Lyngdorf (:84). It extracts Apple iTunNORM tags (via local atoms or millisecond HTTP Range header reads) or live ITU-R BS.1770 LUFS, adds psychoacoustic Crest Factor Compensation for compressed masters, and adjusts your amplifier's hardware attenuator directly.
            

          


          

            
              Does AMR work on Intel Macs as well as Apple Silicon M1 / M2 / M3 / M4?
              +
            
            

              Yes. AppleMusicRemoteMac-Universal.dmg is a true Universal 2 binary containing native arm64 and x86_64 executables for macOS 14.0 Sonoma and newer. On Intel Macs (which lack the Apple Neural Engine), on-device AI curation switches are cleanly hidden with zero CPU overhead while all CoreAudio Bit-Perfect, FFT verification, Room Auto-PEQ, Amplifier TCP, BluOS/DLNA, and WLED features run natively.
